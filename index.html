Lets Make A Tea

Ingredients:

  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Ginger
  • Tea leaves
  • Water
  • Cardamom

Instructions:

  1. Add milk into a vessel and let it boil
  2. Add water if you want.
  3. Add tea leaves in the milk.
  4. Add sugar according to your preferance.
  5. Add grated ginger into the tea
  6. Stir and let it boil for 5-7 minutes.
  7. Pour the tea into the cup with the help of a stainer and enjoy your tea.

Other things you can enjoy your tea with:

  • Fritters
  • Biscuits and cookies
  • Rusk
